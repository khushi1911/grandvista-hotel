Our Hotel Policies

Please review the following policies before making your reservation.

01

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in begins at 2:00 PM and check-out is until 12:00 PM. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability.

02

Cancellation Policy

Cancellation terms may vary depending on the selected booking or offer. Guests are advised to review the applicable cancellation details before confirming a reservation.

03

Guest Identification

Guests may be required to provide valid identification during check-in. The name on the booking should match the identification provided.

04

Smoking Policy

Smoking is not permitted inside guest rooms or enclosed common areas. Guests should use designated smoking areas where available.

05

Pets

Pet policies may vary depending on the room and booking conditions. Please contact the hotel before making a reservation if you are travelling with a pet.

06

Guest Conduct

Guests are expected to respect other guests, hotel staff and hotel property throughout their stay.
